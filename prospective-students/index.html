Prospective PhD Students

I am recruiting PhD students to begin in Fall 2027. Potential projects include:

  • Scalable Neurosymbolic Static Analysis: Augmenting efficient static analysis techniques with neural oracles to extend their expressivity without sacrificing performance.
  • Verification of Randomized Distributed Algorithms: Proving the correctness of critical algorithms, including blockchain consensus and secure multiparty computation schemes.
  • Interoperable Logics: Enabling specifications to be shared across large proof artifacts that span multi-million-line codebases.

For more information, see my research page.

Who should apply

Strong candidates will have a background in programming languages, for example coursework or research experience in programming language theory, type systems, program logics, formal verification, and/or proof assistants such as Rocq or Lean.

Some of my work intersects with AI, but it is grounded in the formal foundations that make verification trustworthy and efficient. I am not interested in projects that simply experiment with LLMs to verify programs in a freeform way. If that is your main interest, I am unlikely to be the right advisor for you.

If you meet these criteria, please reach out to me!
