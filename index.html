About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the Stevens Institute of Technology in Hoboken, NJ. My research focuses on efficient techniques to automate formal verification at scale, and the mathematical foundations needed to apply verification to a wide variety of domains.

I received my PhD from Cornell University in 2026 where I was advised by Alexandra Silva. Before that, I was a staff software engineer in the Facebook Programming Languages and Runtimes team, where I used dependently typed Haskell in production and formally verified concurrent algorithms for an OS microkernel. My research has been recognized with the 2024 ACM SIGPLAN John Vlissides Award for applied software research and a distinguished paper award at POPL 2026.

I am recruiting PhD students with a background in PL theory for the 2027–28 academic year. If you are interested in working with me, you can find more information here.
